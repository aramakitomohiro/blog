## CLI公式仕様の参照範囲

redshift：C002 describe-clusters、C003 describe-cluster-snapshots、C004 describe-snapshot-schedules、C005 describe-storage、C008 describe-logging-status、C009 describe-events、C010 create-cluster-snapshot、C012 restore-table-from-cluster-snapshot、C014 delete-cluster-snapshot、C015 delete-cluster、C020 describe-snapshot-copy-grants

redshift-serverless：C021 list-namespaces、C022 get-namespace、C024 get-workgroup、C025 list-snapshots、C026 get-snapshot、C027 list-recovery-points、C029 list-snapshot-copy-configurations、C031 create-snapshot、C035 restore-table-from-snapshot、C038 delete-snapshot、C040 list-scheduled-actions
